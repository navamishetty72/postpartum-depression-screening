# Research: Literature Review

This directory contains empirical evidence extraction matrices and review summaries from relevant scientific publications across perinatal depression, affective speech computing, and multimodal learning.

## Key Publications Reviewed
- Griffin et al. (2026), *Women's Health Reports* (Multimodal acoustic-linguistic PPD screening).
- Han et al. (2026), *Frontiers in Public Health* (Voice acoustic features & early risk factors for PPD).
- Zhang et al. (2024), *Phenomics* (Text, audio, and video depression detection on DAIC-WOZ).
- He et al. (2024/2026), *Information Fusion* (LMVD vlogger depression benchmark).
- Eyben et al. (2016), *IEEE TAFFC* (Geneva Minimalistic Acoustic Parameter Set - GeMAPS).
- Cox et al. (1987), *British Journal of Psychiatry* (Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale derivation).
- Kaabachi et al. (2025), *npj Digital Medicine* (Medical synthetic data utility and privacy metrics).
